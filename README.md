# COMP3901: CAPSTONE GROUP PROJECT

## Product Context and Need
Healthcare service delivery environments frequently face operational inefficiencies caused by manual scheduling, long waiting times, and limited visibility into resource utilization. The UWI Health Centre currently faces a very similar issue of overcrowding and inefficient phone-based appointment booking.  This current manual system lacks transparency in wait times, provides limited scheduling control, and places significant administrative burden on the clinic staff.

As a team, we proposed a web-based appointment booking and queue management system that applies constraint-based scheduling and decision-support analytics to improve service efficiency and user experience. The main function of this software is to act as a centralized digital platform that facilitates automated notifications, real-time queue monitoring, and structured appointment scheduling. To incorporate this intelligent scheduling logic and rule-based decision support, the appointment allocations will be guided by configurable policies such as appointment duration, buffer times, doctor availability, and historical average service times. 

Additionally, to reduce no-shows and administrative workload, the system will include: automated confirmation and reminder notifications, late cancellation handling and waitlist promotion and lastly an interactive AI chatbot that assists users with booking appointments, viewing availability, and answering common clinic-related questions.


## Product Functionality
The proposed system will provide the following major functionalities:
- Online appointment booking with availability validation
- Doctor schedule and clinic capacity management
- Real-time queue monitoring and wait time estimation
- Automated notifications for confirmations and reminders
- Administrative dashboard with analytics and reports
- AI-powered chatbot for user assistance and system navigation
